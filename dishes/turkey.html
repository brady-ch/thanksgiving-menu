Turkey

Host Day of

Plan

Roast a ~10 lb turkey for 10 guests. Coordinate oven with ham, soufflé, and roll reheats.

Ingredients

  • 1 turkey, ~10 lb, thawed
  • ½ cup butter or oil, softened
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt, 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 4 cups roughly chopped onion, celery, carrot (optional under bird)
  • 2 cups low-sodium turkey stock (pan drippings for gravy — guest gravy from Maddy & Brady)

Oven

Preheat 325°F. Roast until thickest breast registers 165°F (leg ~175°F), typically 2¾–3½ hours for 10 lb. Rest 20–30 minutes before carve. See timeline.
