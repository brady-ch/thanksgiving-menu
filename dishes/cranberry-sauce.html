Cranberry Sauce

Rustekias bring finished sauce

Assignment

Rustekias prepare and bring cranberry sauce. Recipe is theirs; it includes prepared horseradish for bite.

Host shopping: you are not shopping cranberries unless helping — but you may buy a jar of horseradish for their recipe (grocery list).

Typical horseradish cranberry (reference)

  • 24 oz fresh cranberries
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 2–3 tbsp prepared horseradish, to taste
  • Zest of 1 orange (optional)
