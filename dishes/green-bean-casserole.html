Green Bean Casserole

Host Assemble Wed Bake Thu

Ingredients

  • 2 lb green beans, trimmed (or 3 cans cut beans, drained)
  • 2 cans (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups French fried onions (1½ in casserole, ½ on top)

Mix Wednesday; bake 350°F 30 min Thursday; add top onions last 5 min.
