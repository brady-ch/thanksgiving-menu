Gravy

Maddy & Brady Day of

Ingredients

  • ½ cup turkey drippings (or butter + stock if arriving separately)
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 cups low-sodium turkey or chicken stock, warmed
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp black pepper
  • Optional: splash soy sauce or Worcestershire for depth

Make roux with drippings and flour; whisk in stock; simmer until thick. Transport in thermos or slow cooker on low.
