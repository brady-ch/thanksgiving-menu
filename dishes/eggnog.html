Eggnog

Host + Brady · Thursday together · Triple batch (3× base: 6 yolks, 3 cups milk, 1½ cups custard cream, ½ cup sugar, 4 whites, 1 cup whipped cream → scaled below for 10 guests)

Food safety: Cook custard base to 160°F. Swiss meringue heats whites + sugar to 160°F. All eggnog work is Thursday day-of — a group activity for host and Brady (Maddy & Brady still bring rolls, mash, and gravy separately).

Thursday flow (~10:00 AM)

  • ~10:00 AM — host and Brady together (one block; stovetop + chill, not the oven): cook custard to 160°F, strain, ice bath until cold enough to fold.
  • In the same session: Swiss meringue, whip cream, fold; refrigerate until dinner (timeline).

Custard base — start ~10:00 AM (3× batch)

  • 18 large egg yolks (use whites from the same 18 eggs: refrigerate 12 for Thursday meringue; 6 extra whites optional for another use)
  • 9 cups whole milk (Costco)
  • 4½ cups heavy cream (Costco)
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • 3 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tsp freshly grated nutmeg, plus more for garnish

Whisk yolks and sugar. Heat milk, cream, salt, nutmeg until steaming. Temper into yolks; return to pot; stir constantly until mixture reaches 160°F. Strain; cool in ice bath until ready to fold (same ~10:00 AM block).

Finish in the same ~10:00 AM block (3× batch)

  • 12 large egg whites, room temperature
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar (for Swiss meringue)
  • 3 cups heavy cream, cold (whipped to soft peaks)

Swiss meringue: gently heat whites + sugar over simmering water to 160°F while whisking; whip to stiff glossy peaks. Fold meringue and then whipped cream into chilled custard. Serve within a few hours, well chilled.
