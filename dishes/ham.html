Ham

Host Day of only

Costco spiral honey ham

Pre-cooked spiral honey ham from Costco — reheat Thursday only (day of), not Wednesday night beyond purchase and chilling.

Day of

  • Let ham stand 30–45 minutes at room temp.
  • Brush with included glaze or honey-Dijon (¼ cup honey + 2 tbsp Dijon).
  • Cover with foil; heat at 275–300°F until center reaches 140°F, ~10–12 min/lb (often 1½–2 hr for large spiral).
  • Rest 10 min; carve thin slices.
  • For day-of oven planning, block ~1½–2 hours right after soufflé comes out — see timeline.

Slot ham when turkey is resting and after soufflé exits the oven — only one oven slot at a time.
