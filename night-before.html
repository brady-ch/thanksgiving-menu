Night Before

Goal: each family preps about two dishes Wednesday evening so Thursday stays calm. Ham is not cooked Wednesday—only confirm Costco ham is purchased/chilled.

Host — Wednesday (~2 dishes)

  • Assemble green bean casserole through topping layer; refrigerate covered.
  • Dry brine or prep turkey if using (tray, rack, seasoning).

Rustekias — Wednesday (~2 dishes)

  • Sweet potato soufflé: cook and mash sweet potatoes, mix filling, spread in buttered 9×13, add pecan topping; refrigerate covered (no bake) — host bakes Thursday. Recipe.
  • Cranberry sauce: make sauce at home (includes horseradish); chill and bring Thursday, or finish garnish day of — notes.

Maddy & Brady — Wednesday (~2 dishes worth of prep)

  • Shop rolls, potatoes, and gravy ingredients for Thursday (they bring rolls, mashed potatoes, and gravy day of).
  • Optional: peel potatoes and store in water overnight (if making mash Thursday morning at their home before transport).
